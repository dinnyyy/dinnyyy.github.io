JackTen: NLHE Poker Bot WIP

PythonGame TheoryBayesian InferenceEquity / EVLSTMDeep Learning

A no-limit hold'em strategy engine, built from the math up rather than from a library. The roadmap runs hand evaluation and equity first, then the EV, pot odds, and minimum-defense-frequency formulas that drive every decision, then Bayesian range updates and opponent stats (VPIP, PFR, 3-bet%) for exploitative play. I'm currently through the equity and EV layers. Next up is deep learning for opponent behaviour: sequence models (LSTM and, if the data supports it, transformer-based) trained on hand-history action sequences to predict opponent tendencies and ranges, feeding into the same Bayesian updates rather than replacing them.
