Volatility Forecasting with GARCH

A study applying a GARCH(1,1) model to a decade of S&P 500 daily log returns to capture time-varying volatility and clustering. Fitted by maximum likelihood and validated with Ljung-Box and ARCH-LM diagnostics, then used to forecast conditional variance 100 days ahead, automating the full data, evaluation, and reporting pipeline in Python.
